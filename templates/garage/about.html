Our story

More than a garage — a long-term partner for your vehicle.

Evercare Garage was founded to offer dependable, customer-first automotive service for drivers who want quality repairs without the stress. Over the years, we have grown from a small workshop into a trusted garage serving individuals, families, and commercial clients across Nairobi.

We combine experienced workmanship, modern diagnostics, and a practical understanding of the everyday demands drivers face. Whether you need a routine check-up or a more complex repair, our team focuses on accurate diagnosis, transparent communication, and lasting value.
